Our Heritage & Commitment

We’re shaping a world where inclusion is the norm, not an exception.

At CBM India, we believe that disability is part of the rich diversity of human experience. Yet, persons with disabilities often face significant barriers that limit their autonomy and participation in society. For nearly 60 years, we have been working to build an inclusive India. Today, CBM India is a leading NGO for persons with disabilities and other marginalized groups, addressing the root causes that keep them from living a fulfilling and meaningful life.

We build inclusive systems and ensure everyone can enjoy essential services and rights like healthcare, education, decent work, and safe environments.

“

Our vision is simple: a society where persons with disabilities achieve their full potential.

Our Pillars

Our Work

Building an inclusive society starts with promoting equal opportunities. Our core initiatives are at the heart of this mission.

01

Inclusive Education

Ensuring every child with a disability has access to barrier-free neighborhood schools, assistive learning devices, accessible learning materials, and specially trained educators.

  • Accessible STEM laboratories & smart classrooms
  • Screen reader & braille educational material
  • Teacher training in universal design for learning
02

Inclusive Livelihood

Opening pathways to economic independence through market-driven vocational training, digital academies, entrepreneurship seed grants, and corporate job placements.

  • IT Helpdesk & Retail sales certification
  • Micro-enterprise mentorship & seed funding
  • Disability-inclusive workplace sensitization
03

Inclusive Humanitarian Action

Integrating persons with disabilities into disaster preparedness, rapid emergency relief, health responses, and community-led climate resilience programs.

  • Accessible emergency shelters & early warnings
  • Targeted food, dignity & medical kit delivery
  • Disability-disaggregated vulnerability mapping
